# Per-line delivery guidance

Keep every supplied speaker label and spoken word unchanged. Edit only the line's `instruct` field.

Describe the immediate emotional objective, subtext, and performable delivery. Use intensity, pacing, rhythm, articulation, projection, restraint, pauses, or emphasis only when they materially help the line. Keep directions concise and specific. Do not restate the character's persistent register, timbre, accent, anatomy, or other voice identity on every line. Do not add camera directions, scene blocking, unsupported acting inventions, or generic character-summary templates.
