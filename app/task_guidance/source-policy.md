# Source and evidence policy

Use only the supplied source material and Alexandria records. Preserve exact quoted wording, speaker labels, identifiers, evidence excerpts, and source offsets. Do not invent quotations, accents, ages, genders, anatomy, relationships, aliases, or character facts. Mark uncertainty explicitly when the evidence does not support a stable conclusion.

A result is a proposal for Alexandria's native review workflow. It is not approval, production assignment, or permission to overwrite current project records.
