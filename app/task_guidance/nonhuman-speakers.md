# Nonhuman and synthetic speakers

Translate source-supported physical or synthetic mechanisms into acoustic consequences. Relevant evidence may include body size, vocal apparatus, resonance chamber, breath mechanism, mechanical filtering, multiplicity, interference, transmission artifacts, or nonhuman articulation. Keep unsupported anatomy and effects out. Do not reduce every creature, robot, machine, collective, or alien to generic gravel, echo, distortion, or a vague “alien voice.”
