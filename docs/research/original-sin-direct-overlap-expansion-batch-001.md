## Voice-only contract

A direct substitution must contain:

- every intended word, including complete first and final words;
- no sound before or after the intended words;
- no adjacent speaker;
- no background music;
- no sound effects or room spill;
- no echo or separator artifact;
- a transcript-exact production-format MP3 proxy.

Automated transcript and encoding checks cannot prove that music, effects, or
subtle artifacts are absent. Blind listening remains mandatory.
